Your JQL query
project = "YOUR_PROJECT" AND issuetype IN (Story, Bug, Task) AND created >= -30d ORDER BY created DESC
💡 Tip: Remove date filter to export all historical data for trend charts
How to export from Jira
  • 1Go to Jira → click Filters in the top nav
  • 2Click Advanced issue search
  • 3Paste the JQL above into the search bar → hit Enter
  • 4Click Export (top right) → Export CSV (all fields)
  • 5Drag the downloaded file into the upload zone above ↑
